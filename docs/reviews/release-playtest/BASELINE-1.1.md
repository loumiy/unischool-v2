# The 1.1 baseline (Phase 35)

The middle years of the 1.0 game, measured by `npm run balance` before any 1.1 phase changed the economy. Every later 1.1 phase runs the same dashboard and compares its numbers with these. The bands are DD §17's 1.1 guardrails.

| Measure         | What it reads                                                                                                                            | Band                                   |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Cash cover      | Operating cash as years of the year's budgeted expenses, at Years 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50                                                  | 0.25–1.0 years, from Year 20           |
| Sting           | The median of each presented event's costliest choice, as a share of that year's budgeted expenses, per decade                           | 1–5% in every decade                   |
| Saturated years | Years in which any class stood above 95 satisfaction                                                                                     | 0                                      |
| Title rate      | Varsity titles per season played                                                                                                         | 10–25% for a college that fields teams |
| Demand response | The change in the applicant pool four years after every teacher at Year 25 teaches twenty points worse                                   | a drop of at least 10%                 |
| Idle beats      | Calendar beats that stopped the clock with nothing to decide: a sound Board Meeting with no promise to consider, a Convocation with none | 25 or fewer per run                    |
| Tag overlap     | Tags every archetype earned on the same seed                                                                                             | none                                   |

## What it says

- **Money:** the steward holds more than a year of expenses in cash by Year 20 and three to four years by Year 50; the frugal college holds thirty. The growth college runs close to empty, and on one seed finishes in the red.
- **Sting:** the median priced choice costs well under 1% of a year's budget in every decade, for every archetype. Choices rarely cost anything the college notices.
- **Saturation:** the steward has a class above 95 satisfaction for 11–13 of its 50 years, and wins about half of all its seasons.
- **Demand:** four years of teaching twenty points worse moves the applicant pool by less than 4% either way. That is noise, not a response.
- **Beats:** 58–82 calendar beats a run stop the clock with nothing to decide.
- **Identity:** every archetype earns "Artsy" on every seed.

- The three open Phase 21 audit findings (A1 teaching, A2 estate ×2) are promoted from `it.fails` to `it`.
